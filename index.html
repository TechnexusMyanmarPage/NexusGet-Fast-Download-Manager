Privacy Policy

NexusGet is a download manager and web browser published by TechNexus Myanmar. This policy describes information handled by the app and by optional or integrated third-party services.

1. Account and personal information

NexusGet does not require an account to use its core download features and does not ask you to provide your name, phone number, or email address to use those features.

2. Information stored on your device

Download records, app preferences, browser history, bookmarks, and open tabs may be stored locally to provide app features. The app processes the web addresses and file locations you choose to browse, download, open, share, or manage files.

3. Websites and downloads

When you visit a website or download a file, your device connects to that website or server. The service you connect to may receive your IP address and technical request information needed to provide the connection. NexusGet does not operate a server that receives your browsing history, download links, or downloaded files.

4. Advertising and third-party services

NexusGet may display ads using the Start.io advertising SDK. The advertising provider may process technical identifiers, IP address, app or ad interaction information, and diagnostics to deliver, measure, or protect advertising under its own policies. If enabled in a build, Firebase Remote Config may retrieve app configuration, and Firebase Crashlytics may process crash diagnostic information. Third-party services process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Files, permissions, and notifications

The app uses Android file or storage access to save downloads to a destination you select and to open, share, or delete files when you request those actions. Files saved to shared or selected storage may remain there even if you remove an in-app download record or uninstall the app. Notifications and background operation depend on Android permissions and device settings.

6. Your choices and data control

  • Use in-app controls, where available, to manage download records, browser history, and bookmarks.
  • Manage app permissions, notifications, advertising privacy settings, and network access in Android settings.
  • Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove local records and preferences. Separately saved files may need to be removed manually.

7. Children's privacy

NexusGet is a general-purpose utility and is not designed specifically for children. The developer does not knowingly ask children to submit personal information through an account system.

8. Security and policy updates

Reasonable care is taken in designing app data handling, but no device or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. This policy may change when app features or third-party services change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact about privacy

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact technexusmyanmar@gmail.com.

Support & Help

Try the steps below before contacting support.

Downloads won't start or finish

  • Check that your internet connection is stable and the link is still valid.
  • Some websites require sign-in, verification, or a fresh temporary link. Try opening the page in the built-in browser.
  • Open the Queue screen and retry a failed task.
  • Confirm that the selected destination has enough free storage.

Downloads stop in the background

Allow notifications if Android requests permission. Check your device's battery optimization or background activity settings for NexusGet. Manufacturer-specific battery controls may restrict background work.

Can't save, open, or share a file

Check the selected folder and available space. Android may ask you to choose a folder or grant access. If a file was moved or deleted outside NexusGet, check whether it still exists at the original location.

Browser, tabs, and bookmarks

Use the address bar to open a website or search, and the browser menu to manage tabs, history, and bookmarks. Websites may use cookies or require sign-in under their own policies. Browser filtering may not block every ad or tracker.

Report a problem

When contacting support, include your Android version, NexusGet version, what you were trying to do, what happened, and the exact error message if available. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, or private files.
