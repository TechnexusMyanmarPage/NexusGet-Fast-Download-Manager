NexusGet Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

For NexusGet - Fast Download Manager by TechNexus Myanmar

1. Overview

NexusGet is an Android download manager and browser. The app does not require an account. The developer does not operate a service intended to receive your downloaded files, download URLs, browser history or bookmarks. The app and integrated third-party SDKs may process technical information needed for their functions, as explained below.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores download records, settings, browser history and bookmarks locally to provide its features. It processes the URLs and file destinations you choose so it can download, open, share or manage files. The core download features do not ask you to submit your name, phone number or account email.

3. Websites, downloads and BitTorrent

When you browse a website or download from an HTTP/HTTPS server, your device connects to that website or server. The destination may receive your IP address, request details and other information normally required for network communication. When using a magnet link or torrent, your IP address may be visible to other peers and trackers in the torrent swarm. This is inherent to BitTorrent networking and is not hidden by NexusGet. Only download or share content you are authorized to access.

4. Advertising and third-party services

NexusGet integrates the Start.io advertising SDK. Depending on the SDK configuration, provider policies and applicable device settings, the advertising provider may process device identifiers, IP address, app/ad interaction information and diagnostic data to deliver, measure or protect advertising. Firebase Remote Config may be included for remote ad configuration when enabled by the developer in a build; it may communicate with Google services to retrieve configuration. Third-party providers are responsible for their own data practices. Please review their current privacy policies and your device's privacy/ad settings.

5. Files, permissions and background activity

The app uses Android storage and file-picker access to save downloads to a destination you select and to open, share or delete files when you request those actions. Files saved to shared or user-selected storage may remain after you remove a download record or uninstall the app and may need to be deleted separately. Notifications and background execution support user-started downloads where Android and device battery settings permit.

6. Your choices and data control

You can manage or delete download records, browser history and bookmarks using controls provided in the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove app-local records and settings. Files in shared or user-selected storage may remain. You can manage app permissions, notifications, advertising/privacy options and network access through Android settings.

7. Children

NexusGet is a general-purpose utility and is not designed specifically for children. The developer does not knowingly request personal information from children through an account system. Integrated third-party SDKs may have their own age-related requirements and data practices.

8. Security and policy changes

No software or network transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. This policy may be updated when app features or third-party services change. The revision date at the top indicates the latest published version.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, requests or support, contact technexusmyanmar@gmail.com.
